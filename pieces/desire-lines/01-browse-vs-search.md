# Browse vs search: the route through the tree

Alt: A grid of thin grey lines. A dark ink stroke leaves the top-left corner, makes four right-angle turns through the grid, and ends at the bottom-right. A dashed rust line cuts straight across the grid on the diagonal between the same two points.

Notes: Drawn after Ines Gridlock's question in Streets & Systems ("Which desire paths do you actually use here?"). The solid line is the browse route: community by community, one stroke, no curves, only the grid. The dashed line is the search shortcut, which is not a drawn route at all but a jump. Constraint for this piece: one stroke, orthogonal only. The dashed line breaks it on purpose, and that is the point: the shortcut is the only thing on the page with a pen lift in it.
